Basketball Park Support

Ten basketball challenges in a living park. Take a shot, follow the arc and build your best star ratings.

Start a challenge

Tap a numbered attraction on the map to select and animate it, then tap the small orange Play button beside its number to begin a 60-second round. Your chosen player and four animated friends gather near the selected attraction. The map remembers the last challenge actually played; first launch defaults to Park Court (1). Swipe up on the scene to shoot. Adjust swipe length for power and direction for aim. Tap L, C or R to move the shooter. Use Pause to resume, replay or return to the park.

Shooting help

Tap the circular i on the map or Help during a round for a replayable animated shooting coach. The dotted guide uses grey/light green for short shots, dark green for an on-target estimate, and red for excess power. Very tall previews are fitted on screen for readability. The guide does not guarantee a basket: rims, moving hoops, the train window frame and scenery can change the shot.

Stars and settings

Make 2, 4, 6, 8 or 10 baskets in one round to earn one through five stars. The map retains the best rating per challenge. Open the Settings gear to choose Maya, Leo, Zoe, Kai or Alex and toggle speech, sound effects and music independently. Settings and progress stay on your device; uninstalling can remove them. No cloud sync is provided.

Availability and purchases

Basketball Park is an iPhone game. All ten challenges and five players are included. There are no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases, and gameplay needs no account or internet connection.

Report a problem

Use the game support tracker and begin the title with “Basketball Park”. Include the version/build, device, iOS version and what happened. Reports are public: do not include passwords, Apple credentials or sensitive personal information. Children should ask a parent or guardian for help.
