Basketball Park Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

About the game

Basketball Park is an offline basketball game for iPhone, maintained by Beit Solutions LLC. No game account is required.

Data handled by the app

The current game does not include advertising, third-party analytics, tracking, chat or user-uploaded content. It does not request location, contacts, microphone, camera or photo-library access. The developer operates no game backend and does not receive your scores, saved stars, player selection or audio preferences from the app.

Local progress and audio

Scores, best stars, last challenge played, tutorial state, player choice and audio settings are stored on your device. Deleting the app can remove local data; operating-system backups may restore it. There is no developer cloud sync. Character dialogue and background audio are bundled files played on device. Speech is not recorded or sent to a voice service.

Apple services and TestFlight

Apple distributes the app and may process App Store, TestFlight and operating-system diagnostics according to your Apple settings and Apple’s privacy policy. The game does not add a developer analytics service or advertising identifier.

Optional purchases in version 1.3

Apple processes purchases, payment details, refunds and restoration through StoreKit. The app reads verified purchase entitlements on your device to unlock purchased themes and challenges. The developer does not receive your Apple Account credentials or payment-card details and operates no purchase-validation server. An internet connection is needed to buy or restore. Apple handles transaction information under its own privacy policy.

Support website

This support site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web request information under its privacy statement. If you voluntarily post a support issue, the information you submit is public on GitHub. Do not post sensitive information. Website support requests are separate from gameplay data.

Children and choices

The game has no game account, chat or behavioral advertising. Version 1.3 offers optional permanent in-app purchases through Apple. A parent or guardian should help children with public support requests. You can mute speech, sound effects and music independently in Settings. Remove the app to remove its local data, subject to device backups.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support tracker with “Basketball Park privacy” in the title. Do not include private information in a public issue.
