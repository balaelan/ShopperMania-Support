Basketball Park Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

About the game

Basketball Park supports offline solo play, same-phone pass-and-play and optional online Game Center matches for iPhone and room-code matches for Android, maintained by Beit Solutions LLC. No separate sign-up is required; Android online play creates an anonymous technical account.

Data handled by the app

The game does not include advertising, advertising tracking, chat or user-uploaded content. Android online services process the technical information described below. It does not request location, contacts, microphone, camera or photo-library access. Solo progress, best stars and settings remain on your device. Android online play uses a developer-configured Unity Gaming Services project; live match information passes through Unity Relay to participants. We do not maintain a separate match-history database.

Local progress and audio

Scores, best stars, last challenge played, tutorial state, player choice, selected computer difficulty and audio settings are stored on your device. Deleting the app can remove local data; operating-system backups may restore it. There is no developer cloud sync. Character dialogue and background audio are bundled files played on device. Speech is not recorded or sent to a voice service.

Apple services and TestFlight

Apple distributes the app and may process App Store, TestFlight and operating-system diagnostics according to your Apple settings and Apple’s privacy policy. The game does not add a developer analytics service or advertising identifier.

iPhone online play

Online play uses Apple Game Center for sign-in, invitations and connectivity. Your Game Center player identifier and display name, character choice, readiness, match scores and (for version 1.5 or later side-by-side online play) shot/ball motion are exchanged with other match participants through GameKit for the session. Other participants see your display name and scores. The developer operates no multiplayer server, stores no match records on a developer service, and adds no chat or voice chat. Same-phone participant names are session-only. The computer opponent runs on your device without Game Center or a developer network service. Online play requires internet and Game Center; solo and same-phone play do not. Apple processes Game Center information under its privacy policy and your Game Center settings.

Android online play and Unity services

For Android users who select an age range of 13 or older, choosing online play initializes Unity Authentication and creates or resumes an anonymous player identifier without asking for a name, email or password. Unity and its service providers process this identifier and an installation identifier for authentication and account management. A session token is stored on your device to resume the anonymous account.

Unity Relay processes network addresses, approximate region derived from IP address, connection activity and performance information to route and operate online matches and measure service performance. This does not use device GPS or a location permission. Live character selection, readiness, scores, round timing and ball/character motion are sent through encrypted connections and exchanged with your room participants. Android rooms use generic Host/Friend labels and have no chat or voice recording. Online play is optional; solo, computer and same-phone modes do not require these services. Android room-code play and iPhone Game Center play are separate.

Unity handles service data under its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, including applicable operational, security and legal retention. Live game messages are used for the match; service connection records may be retained by Unity. We do not use this information for advertising.

Delete your Android online account

In the Android app, open Settings, scroll to Privacy & online data, choose Delete anonymous online account, and confirm. Internet is required. This requests deletion of the Unity Authentication account and clears its local session. Selecting online play afterward creates a new anonymous account. It does not delete device stars, settings or store purchases. Operational Relay records may be retained according to Unity's policy and legal requirements.

For help requesting deletion outside the app, email beitsolutionsllc@gmail.com with the subject “Basketball Park Android data deletion.” Do not send passwords, payment information or session tokens. We will help identify the relevant anonymous account securely; please keep the app installed until the request is resolved. Apple Game Center data is managed through Apple settings.

Free content and earlier purchases

Starting with version 1.9, all six city themes and all ten challenges are free on iPhone and Android. The app does not start the store billing connection or offer purchases, and no restoration or reviewer code is needed. Earlier versions may contain the optional purchases described below; their transaction records remain with the platform store.

Apple processes iPhone purchases through StoreKit; Google Play processes Android purchases, payment details, refunds and restoration through Play Billing. The app reads verified purchase entitlements on your device to unlock purchased themes and challenges. The developer does not receive your Apple or Google Account credentials or payment-card details and operates no purchase-validation server. An internet connection is needed to buy or restore. Each store handles transaction information under its own privacy policy. Android purchase records are read and verified on the device to unlock permanent products; no developer purchase-validation server is used.

Support website

This support site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web request information under its privacy statement. If you voluntarily post a support issue, the information you submit is public on GitHub. Do not post sensitive information. Website support requests are separate from gameplay data.

Children, age ranges and choices

The game supports school-age children, teens and adults. Android presents a neutral age-range selector before online rooms. The selected range is stored only on the device, and is not sent to Unity or the developer; no birth date is requested. Under-13 and unknown-age users cannot initialize Unity online play. They can use solo, computer opponents and same-phone pass-and-play. Under-13 selections may be checked again after one year. Android online rooms are available to ages 13 and older. The game has no chat or behavioral advertising; online play uses the anonymous technical account described above. Version 1.9 has no in-app purchase offers. A parent or guardian should help children with public support requests. You can mute speech, sound effects and music independently in Settings. Remove the app to remove its local data, subject to device backups.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support tracker with “Basketball Park privacy” in the title. Do not include private information in a public issue.
