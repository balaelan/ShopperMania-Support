Aztecs Run Support

Escape rolling boulders, dodge spears and collect ancient relics through jungle ruins.

How to play

  • Swipe left or right to change lanes.
  • Swipe up to jump and catch an overhead vine. Vines protect against boulders; spears can still hit you.
  • Swipe down to slide under incoming spears.
  • Tap a branch idol to grab it while running.

First launch and tutorial

Choose Learn to play on first launch for safe, guided practice with lane changes, vine jumping, sliding and idol collection. Skip for now takes you to Home. Replay anytime with Tutorial on Home. Practice does not change your scores, bests, rewards or run history. Free tutorial and gameplay do not need a game account. iOS build 53 and later include all explorers and environments for free and do not connect to a purchase service.

Settings and progress

Choose Original or Cartoon visuals and toggle Sound & music on Home. Choose Riven, Kai, Mara, Zara, Vih or Navy through Explorer or Explorers. Recent runs shows your last ten results. Adventure journal includes missions, relics, gear, routes and optional Game Center friends. A paused run ends after ten seconds unless resumed.

Included content

In iOS build 53 and later, all six available explorers and all four environments are free. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads. Outfits and mastery rewards are earned through play. Choose an Environment on Home; scenery stays fixed unless you explicitly change it at a route choice. Older builds, including the current Android test release, may still show optional purchases; use your platform store for help with those transactions.

Game Center

For leaderboards, connect to Game Center in iOS Settings and check your network connection. Core gameplay works without signing in. Local game progress does not currently sync between devices.

Report a problem

Use our existing game support tracker and begin the title with “Aztecs Run”. Include the app version/build, device model, operating-system version and what happened. A screenshot can help. Reports are public: do not include passwords, store credentials or other sensitive information. Children should ask a parent or guardian for help.

For data-handling information, read the Aztecs Run privacy policy.
