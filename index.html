Shopper Mania Support

Shopper Mania is a grocery memory game for iPhone and iPad. Study your shopping list, collect the matching groceries, and see how well you remembered.

Contact support

Report a problem or ask a question through our GitHub support tracker. A GitHub account is required to create a report.

Create a support request

Reports are public. Do not include passwords, phone numbers, email addresses, payment information, or other private details. Use your device model, iOS version, app version, and steps to reproduce the problem. Redact personal information from screenshots.

How to play

  1. Choose a shopping trip and study the grocery list.
  2. Start shopping and tap the groceries from your list.
  3. After collecting an item, tap Done to finish early, or continue until the 60-second timer ends.
  4. Check your cart score, then choose Play Again or Main Menu.

Trips, themes, and purchases

Start with the free trips and markets shown in the app. Additional trips and themes are optional, separate, one-time Apple in-app purchases. Unlocking a theme does not unlock a trip, and unlocking a trip does not unlock a theme. The purchase screen shows Apple’s price for your country or region.

Restore purchases: On Home, choose Browse for More, then Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchases. A network connection is required. Reinstalling the app does not remove a permanent Apple purchase.

Purchase help: If a payment is pending, access starts after Apple confirms it. If an item remains locked after a completed purchase, restore purchases and restart the app. Apple manages payments and refund requests; never post receipts or payment details in public support reports.

Connection: Purchases, restoration, and refreshing the free-content and trip settings need an internet connection. Available gameplay can be played offline.

Common questions

Sound: Use the music and sound controls on the home screen. Also check your device volume.

Orientation: Portrait and landscape are supported. Check your device’s rotation-lock setting if the screen does not rotate.

Update problems: Install the latest available App Store or TestFlight version, then restart the app. If the problem persists, report the steps above.

Privacy: Read the privacy policy for local settings and support-report information.
