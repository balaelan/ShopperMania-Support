Shopper Mania Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

About the app

Shopper Mania is an offline grocery memory game maintained by the developer using the GitHub account balaelan.

Data collected by the game

The game does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer. It does not include active advertising, analytics, cloud diagnostics, tracking, messaging, or in-app purchases.

Local settings

Your store-theme, music, and sound-effect preferences are stored on your device. They are not sent to the developer. Removing the app removes its locally stored settings. Device backups, if enabled, are managed by your operating system.

Support requests

If you choose to contact support through GitHub Issues, your GitHub username and the information you submit are visible to the developer and the public. Do not post sensitive or private information. The developer uses submitted information to investigate issues and respond to requests.

Support reports and their discussion history remain on GitHub until removed under GitHub’s controls and policies. Closing an issue does not delete it. To request removal of information from a support report, contact the maintainer in the tracker without reposting the private information, or use GitHub’s privacy support process. GitHub may retain records under its own policies.

Apple and GitHub services

Apple may process App Store, TestFlight, or device diagnostic information according to Apple’s settings and policies. GitHub hosts this website and support tracker and may process website access information and account activity. These services operate under their own privacy policies: Apple Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children’s privacy

The game does not collect personal information from players, including children. Support requests are optional and use a separate GitHub service; children should ask a parent or guardian for help with support.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated if the app’s privacy practices change. For questions about this policy, use the support tracker. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public report.
