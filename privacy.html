Shopper Mania Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

About the app

Shopper Mania is a grocery memory game for iPhone, iPad, and Android. All eight currently available trips and all eight currently available markets are free; no purchase is required to play them. The app retains store purchase and restoration features for existing purchases. It is maintained by Beit Solutions LLC, using the GitHub account balaelan.

Data collected by the game

The game does not require a game account. It does not include advertising, behavioral analytics, tracking, messaging, or microphone recording. It does not use a gameplay analytics SDK; technical service diagnostics are described below. Your memory lists, selections, and scores are not sent to the developer.

Purchases and restoration

The app uses Apple’s App Store framework to request product information and check Apple-verified purchase entitlements on your device, including previously purchased themes or trips. It supports restoring existing purchases with the same Apple Account. All currently available trips and markets are free. Any store payment processing is handled by Apple. The developer does not receive your Apple Account password or payment-card details, and the app does not send purchase records to the developer’s content-settings server. Apple may provide the developer with sales and financial reports under Apple’s services.

Google Play purchases on Android

On Android, the app connects to Google Play’s billing service to query product information and existing purchases. It verifies Google-signed purchase records on your device and acknowledges purchases with Google Play when needed. Existing purchases can be restored using the same Google Account. All currently available trips and markets are free. Purchase records are processed on your device and by Google Play; they are not sent to the developer’s content-settings server. The developer does not receive your Google Account password or payment-card details. Google may provide the developer with order, sales, and financial reports under its services.

Temporary Android reviewer access

An optional reviewer-access tool remains available for authorized app reviewers. It is not needed to play the currently free trips and markets. If a reviewer enters a developer-provided code, the app sends it by HTTPS POST to the developer’s HostGator-hosted service for validation. The app clears the entry and keeps successful access only in memory, for up to two hours or until the app closes. The server validates against a stored hash of the code. To limit repeated guesses, it stores a salted hash of the requesting IP address, the start time of the attempt window, and an attempt count. Each attempt window stops applying after 15 minutes; expired entries are removed when a subsequent reviewer-verification request is processed and may remain stored until then. Ordinary hosting access logs are handled as described below. Review access does not create a purchase.

Character speech

Character greetings use built-in text. iPhone and iPad use installed system speech voices, and Android uses an available installed, offline English text-to-speech voice. The game does not ask for microphone access or record players’ voices.

Online content settings

The app requests a public settings file from balaelan.com, hosted by HostGator. This file specifies which built-in trips and themes are free and how many groceries appear in a memory list. Requests do not include your name, email address, shopping list, score, purchase history, or Apple or Google credentials. As with ordinary HTTPS connections, the hosting service records technical access-log information such as your IP address, request time, requested settings URL, response status, and app user-agent. The developer and hosting provider can access these logs for operating, securing, and diagnosing the settings service. They are not used to build gameplay profiles, infer location, or advertise to players. Access-log retention and rotation follow the hosting service’s settings and policies; we do not promise immediate deletion of each request.

Local settings

Your selected store theme, music and sound-effect preferences, tutorial progress, and last valid content-settings file are stored on your device. They are not sent to the developer. Your memory lists, selected groceries, and scores are processed on the device and are not uploaded to the developer. Removing the app removes its local game settings, subject to any operating-system backup or restore settings. Permanent purchases remain associated with the Apple Account or Google Account used to buy them and can be restored on that platform. Purchases do not transfer between Apple and Google stores. Device backups, if enabled, are managed by your operating system.

Support requests

If you choose to contact support through GitHub Issues, your GitHub username and the information you submit are visible to the developer and the public. If you email contact@beitsolutionsllc.com, the developer receives your email address and the message and attachments you choose to send. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information. The developer uses support information to investigate issues and respond to requests.

Support correspondence is retained until deleted through support handling or an applicable deletion request, subject to security or legal retention needs and the relevant hosting or email provider’s policies. GitHub support reports and their discussion history remain on GitHub until removed under GitHub’s controls and policies. Closing an issue does not delete it. To request removal, use the privacy contact below and identify the report without reposting private information. GitHub may retain records under its own policies.

Apple, Google, GitHub, and hosting services

Apple may process App Store, TestFlight, or device diagnostic information according to Apple’s settings and policies. Google may process Play Store purchases, billing-service operation, testing, and device diagnostic information under Google’s settings and policies. GitHub hosts this website and support tracker and may process website access information and account activity. HostGator provides hosting for the public content-settings endpoint and processes connection information under its own policies. These services operate under their own privacy policies: Apple Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy, GitHub Privacy Statement, and Newfold Digital / HostGator Privacy Center.

Children’s privacy

The game does not ask players, including children, to create a game account or provide their name, email address, contacts, photos, or location. It has no advertising, behavioral analytics, messaging, or microphone recording. Technical connection information, including IP addresses, is handled as described under Online content settings. All currently available trips and markets are free. Parents or guardians should assist children with any support request or store-related activity and can use the device’s family purchase-approval controls.

Request deletion of data

For Shopper Mania data handled by Beit Solutions LLC, email contact@beitsolutionsllc.com with the subject “Shopper Mania data deletion” and describe the support report or other information you want removed. A parent or guardian may make a request for a child. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or the reviewer code. We will review the request, remove identifiable developer-controlled information where applicable, and explain anything that must be retained for security or legal reasons. We cannot match anonymous gameplay to a person because lists, picks, and scores stay on the device.

Uninstalling the app removes local game settings. Reviewer attempt windows stop applying after 15 minutes; the corresponding salted IP-hash records are removed when a subsequent reviewer-verification request is processed and may remain stored until then. Temporary review access ends after at most two hours or when the app closes. Hosting access logs follow the hosting provider’s retention and rotation settings and may remain until rotation; immediate deletion of individual log entries is not guaranteed. Google Play or App Store purchase records remain with the relevant store under its retention rules. GitHub support reports follow GitHub’s deletion controls and policies; for a public report, identify its link without reposting private information.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated if the app’s privacy practices change. For privacy questions or requests about data handled by the developer, email contact@beitsolutionsllc.com. For ordinary game issues, use the support tracker. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public report.
