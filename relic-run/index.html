Relic Run Support

Escape rolling boulders, dodge spears and collect ancient relics through jungle ruins.

How to play

  • Swipe left or right to change lanes.
  • Swipe up to jump and catch an overhead vine. Vines protect against boulders; spears can still hit you.
  • Swipe down to slide under incoming spears.
  • Tap a branch idol to grab it while running.

First launch and tutorial

Choose Learn to play on first launch for safe, guided practice with lane changes, vine jumping, sliding and idol collection. Skip for now takes you to Home. Replay anytime with Tutorial on Home. Practice does not change your scores, bests, rewards or run history. Free tutorial and gameplay do not need an Apple Account sign-in. The App Store connects when you open an explorer or environment purchase selector.

Settings and progress

Choose Original or Cartoon visuals and toggle Sound & music on Home. Choose Riven, Kai, Mara or Zara through Explorer or Explorers. Recent runs shows your last ten results. Adventure journal includes missions, relics, gear, routes and optional Game Center friends. A paused run ends after ten seconds unless resumed.

Environments and purchases

Choose an Environment on Home before starting. Your chosen background stays fixed for the whole run. Jungle Trail and Temple Ruins are free. Torchlit Cavern, Lost Causeway, Kai and Zara are separate permanent in-app purchases, each priced at US $0.99. Riven and Mara are free; there are no subscriptions. Apple shows the final localized price before payment. Use Restore purchases in the environment or explorer selector with the same Apple Account to recover purchased access. Purchases do not transfer local game progress between devices. Refunds and purchase approvals are handled by Apple.

Game Center

For leaderboards, connect to Game Center in iOS Settings and check your network connection. Core gameplay works without signing in. Local game progress does not currently sync between devices.

Report a problem

Use our existing game support tracker and begin the title with “Relic Run”. Include the app version/build, device model, iOS version and what happened. A screenshot can help. Reports are public: do not include passwords, Apple credentials or other sensitive information. Children should ask a parent or guardian for help.

For data-handling information, read the Relic Run privacy policy.
