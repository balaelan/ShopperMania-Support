Relic Run Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

About Relic Run

Relic Run is a jungle adventure game for iPhone, iPad and Android, maintained by Beit Solutions LLC. Core gameplay works without a game account or Game Center sign-in.

Information handled by the game

The current release contains no advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDK. It does not request your location, contacts, microphone or photo library. The developer does not operate a game backend or receive your saved progress, scores or character selections from the app.

Local progress

Settings, character choice, scores, recent runs, relic collection, missions and outfit progress are stored on your device. Deleting the app removes locally stored app data; operating-system backups may restore it. Relic Run does not provide developer cloud backup or cross-device progress sync.

Optional Apple Game Center (iOS only)

If you connect to Game Center, Apple handles authentication, player identity, friends and leaderboards. The game submits distance, idol and close-call scores to Game Center for ranking. Apple processes this information under its own privacy policy and your Game Center settings. The game does not send it to a developer-operated server. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Optional in-app purchases

Apple StoreKit handles payments and verifies permanent character and environment unlocks. The app reads Apple-verified purchase entitlements on your device to provide access, restore purchases, and honor refunds or revocations. The developer does not receive your payment-card details or send individual transaction records to a developer-operated server. Apple processes purchase information under its own privacy policy. Purchase access can be restored using the same Apple Account; local game progress remains device-specific.

Google Play purchases (Android)

On Android, Google Play Billing handles optional purchases of permanent character and environment unlocks. The app reads purchase receipts and verifies their signatures on the device to grant access, restore purchases, and refresh ownership. Pending payments do not unlock content. The developer does not receive payment-card details or send individual purchase records to a developer-operated server. Google processes store, payment and service information under Google’s privacy policy. Google Play purchases restore through the same Google account and do not transfer to Apple purchases. Android uses local weekly records and does not integrate Google Play Games leaderboards.

Sharing results

Choosing Share result creates a game-result image on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. On Android, sharing opens the system share sheet with the result text; the result image is not attached. Sharing with another app or person occurs only when you choose to do so. The game does not automatically send these images to the developer.

Website and support

GitHub hosts this website and the support tracker. GitHub may process website access information under its privacy statement. If you file a support report, your GitHub username and anything you post are visible to the developer and the public. Include “Relic Run” in the title. Do not post private or sensitive information.

Support reports and their discussion history remain on GitHub until removed under GitHub’s controls and policies. Closing a report does not delete it. To request removal of information, contact the maintainer in the tracker without reposting sensitive information, or use GitHub’s privacy-support process. GitHub may retain records under its own policies.

Children

The game does not request personal information from players, including children. Parents or guardians should help children with optional store purchases, Game Center, sharing or support services, which are subject to those services’ own settings and policies.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app’s practices change, including before introducing advertising or other data-collection features. For privacy questions, use the support tracker and identify the app as Relic Run. Do not include sensitive information in a public report.
